Methodology

How to read this dashboard

Question 1 · Which genres return the most?

Each genre is shown as a box plot on a logarithmic ROI scale. The pale blue box spans the middle half of films, the dark line marks the median, and the whiskers show the observed range. Blue dots represent individual films; a point higher on the chart means a larger return. Scroll or pinch to zoom, then drag vertically to inspect extreme values. Click a genre or its label to apply that genre as a filter.

Question 2 · When should a movie launch?

This heatmap groups films by release month and decade. Each cell's blue intensity represents median ROI: darker blue means a higher median return, while light gray indicates that no qualifying films are available. The number in a cell is the film count. Read the months on the vertical axis and the full decades along the bottom; hover a cell for its detailed summary.

Question 3 · Which creative partners perform?

Directors are ranked by median ROI, with names on the left and ROI on a logarithmic horizontal axis. Dot size indicates the number of films, and the red-to-yellow-to-green color scale represents the share of profitable films. The dashed vertical line marks break-even at 1× ROI. Click a director to inspect their films and frequent cast collaborators.

All three views respond to the genre, budget tier, release season, and minimum-votes filters above. ROI is worldwide revenue divided by budget; movies missing either value are excluded, and revenue is nominal box office revenue.
